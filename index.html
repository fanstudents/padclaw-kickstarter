Imagine a screen that just... helps.

No apps to download. No accounts to create. No prompts to write.

You turn it on, and it already knows what you need.

PadClaw is a 10-inch tablet with one job: be the most helpful thing in your home.

It sits on your kitchen counter, your bedside table, or near your front door. And instead of showing you 147 apps competing for your attention, it shows you one thing: a friendly AI assistant that talks to you like a person.

Ask it anything. Tell it what you need. Or just let it take care of the small things you keep forgetting — so you can focus on the things that actually matter.

The Problem with "Smart" Devices

Let's be honest. AI is everywhere — and yet, for most people, it's completely useless.

  • ChatGPT needs you to be a prompt engineer. Most people open it, stare at the blank box, and close it.
  • Your phone is a dopamine trap. You unlock it to check the weather. 20 minutes later you're doom-scrolling.
  • Smart speakers can't DO anything. "Alexa, book me a dentist appointment." Sorry, I can't do that.
  • AI wearables failed spectacularly. Humane AI Pin: discontinued. Rabbit R1: refund wave. No screen. No browser. No point.
  • Your parents still can't use their iPad. Too many apps. Too small text. Too many passwords.

The gap is enormous: AI is powerful, but inaccessible. Phones are accessible, but overwhelming.

There's no device that simply does things for you without asking you to become a power user first. That's exactly what PadClaw is.

Not an App. A Conversation.

When you turn on PadClaw, you don't see a grid of icons. You don't see a settings page.

You see your AI assistant — ready to talk.

It greets you by name. It knows your schedule. It remembers that you take your medication at 8 AM, that you're learning Spanish, that you like to check your horoscope with your morning coffee, and that you hate getting caught in the rain.

You don't tap through menus. You just say:

"Hey, what should I wear today?"

"Remind me to take my pills after breakfast."

"Practice some Spanish with me."

"What does my chart say about today?"

And it just... does it. No app to open. No subscription to activate. No learning curve to climb.

If you can talk to a person, you can use PadClaw.

Your Data Never Leaves This Device

PadClaw processes everything on-device. There is no cloud backend. There is no user database. We architecturally cannot see your data — because we never built the infrastructure to collect it.

  • On-device AI processing. Your conversations, health data, and personal preferences stay on the tablet.
  • Works offline. Medication reminders, cached weather, language practice — all work without internet.
  • Zero data collection. No telemetry. No ad tracking. No "improving our services." We literally can't see your data.
