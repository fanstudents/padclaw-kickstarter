結論與建議 Conclusions & Recommendations

✅

$599 Price Point is Optimal

The $599 KS price sits at the top of the highest-converting $300–$599 bracket (24% success rate), avoiding the steep drop-off at $600+ (18%). This is the maximum revenue per unit within the high-conversion zone.

✅

"No Subscription" is the #1 Differentiator

Every competitor is moving toward subscription models. PadClaw's zero-subscription promise is a contrarian, powerful positioning — especially as subscription fatigue reaches all-time highs.

✅

Sep/Oct Launch Window

September–October KS launch aligns with holiday gift-buying intent, media coverage cycles, and historically highest-performing campaign months. Target Feb fulfillment for Valentine's gifting wave.

✅

Privacy Story Resonates

On-device AI processing directly addresses the 78% of consumers concerned about cloud AI data collection. This isn't a feature — it's a market positioning weapon.

✅

Dual Persona Revenue Strategy

Lead with Tech-Forward Families (35% of TAM, highest volume), then upsell with Duo Pack bundles for couples/multigenerational households to maximize revenue per backer.

⚠️

Risk is Manageable

Primary risks (manufacturing delays, shipping costs) are mitigated by an existing 4-year factory relationship with pre-negotiated production slots. No unknowns in the supply chain.
