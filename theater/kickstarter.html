Why we built it

Barbara has a smartphone.
She hates it.

Her daughter Emily set up every app, every account, every reminder. Barbara still calls to ask about the weather — because the phone answers with forty icons and a login screen.

So we built a screen that answers with a sentence. It sits on the counter, hears her from across the kitchen, and shows exactly one thing at a time — in letters she can read from the doorway.

"I don't need another app. I need something that just works without me having to think about it."

— from 214 interviews with US families, 2025–2026

Not an app. A conversation.

Say it out loud.
That's the whole manual.

"What should I wear today?" — "Light jacket. Rain at eight." The microphones are tuned for a voice from four meters away, over a running faucet. The screen shows what it heard, in big type, so Barbara knows it got it right.

And when it's done, it goes quiet. No feed to scroll, no autoplay, no red badges. PadClaw is designed to be finished with.

Risks & challenges

The honest part

Hardware is hard, and we won't pretend otherwise. Our biggest risks are certification timing (FCC/UL can add 4–6 weeks) and display panel allocation. We've mitigated both: certifications begin during the campaign with pre-screened test units, and our panel is a mature, multi-source part — not a custom cut.

The software you saw in the demo is planned software: real design, real voice pipeline, sample data. If a channel slips, the device still ships on time — channels arrive over the air. We will publish a monthly build log, including the ugly weeks.

FAQ

What happens after the 6 free months?

Nothing bad. The device keeps working forever — on-device AI stays unlimited and free. If you want the private-cloud extras (long reasoning, chart maths, translation), the Family plan is $14/month, locked for life for founders. No auto-renew; we ask you 14 days before.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Sensitive tasks run on-device or in our own Oregon racks, logs stay on your device for 90 days, and one button wipes everything — device and servers. We sell hardware, not attention.

Can it use my own AI account?

Yes — bring your own OpenAI, Gemini or Anthropic key in Settings. Sensitive tasks still stay on PadClaw AI unless you explicitly turn that off.

Why only 3,000 units?

One production line, one batch, every unit numbered and hand-checked. Small enough for a two-person hardware team to do properly; large enough to fund the tooling. When they're gone, they're gone.
